What is Helbaron?

Helbaron is a free Markdown editor for Windows, macOS and Linux. You write on a calm page that looks like a finished document, while your work is saved as plain .md files on your own disk. It opens and exports Word documents, renders tables, math and diagrams as you type, and handles Arabic and mixed Arabic–English text properly. It is created by Hassan Maged Elbaron.

Features

A page, not a text box

Headings, lists, tables, images, KaTeX math and Mermaid diagrams render live. Click any block to edit its source.

Word in, Word out

Open .docx files and keep editing them as Markdown. Export to Word, PDF or a standalone web page.

Arabic done properly

Every paragraph picks its own direction, Arabic text gets tuned typography, and search ignores harakat: احمد finds أحمد.

Five styles, light and dark

Baron, Glass, Slate, Paper and Minimal. Choose your look on first launch with a live preview.

Your files, your machine

No account and no cloud. Tabs, file tree, workspace search, auto-save, version history and optional WebDAV sync.

Optional AI assistant

Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and local models. Off until you add a provider; every edit is a preview first.

The best Markdown editor for Arabic and RTL writing

Most Markdown editors set one direction for the whole document, so mixed Arabic and English text breaks. Helbaron gives each paragraph its own direction automatically, removes letter-spacing from Arabic, adds line height for diacritics, keeps Arabic paragraphs right-to-left in exported Word files, and offers a full Arabic interface. Search folds hamza, alef maqsura and ta marbuta: مدرسه finds مدرسة.

Frequently asked questions

Is Helbaron free?

Yes. Helbaron is completely free and open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license. There are no paid tiers, no account and no subscription.

Can Helbaron open and export Word documents?

Yes. Open .docx files from the app or straight from your file manager and keep editing them as Markdown. Export to Word (.docx), PDF or HTML; Arabic paragraphs stay right-to-left in Word.

Which operating systems does Helbaron support?

Windows 10 and 11, macOS on Apple Silicon and Intel, and Linux (.AppImage, .deb and .rpm).

Does Helbaron need an account or internet?

No. It works offline and keeps documents as ordinary Markdown files on your disk. Optional sync and AI features only connect to services you configure.

How is Helbaron different from Typora, Obsidian and MarkText?

It is free and open source unlike Typora, builds in Word import and export, and has per-paragraph Arabic and RTL direction with Arabic-aware search, which the others support only partially.

Who made Helbaron?

Helbaron is created by Hassan Maged Elbaron. It is built on Markra, an open-source Markdown editor, and keeps its copyright notices.
